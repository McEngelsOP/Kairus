How to use accounts

  1. Create your accounts

    Add cash, bank accounts, savings or cards. Use Asset for money you have and Liability for debts such as a credit card.

  2. Pick an account when you add

    The account button next to the amount sets where the money comes from or goes to. Its balance updates by itself.

  3. Move money with Transfer

    Use the Transfer tab to move money between accounts, for example from Cash to Savings or to pay a card. Transfers are not expenses.

  4. Check your net worth

    Reports > Accounts shows assets minus liabilities. Tap any account to edit it.
